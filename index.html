Where the money goes

In 2024–25, residential care took $24.2 billion (60.8%); home care and support took $12.9 billion (32.4%). Each square is about 1%.

Source: Australian Government aged care expenditure, 2024–25.

So, is care keeping up?

  • Demand is rising. The 65+ share keeps growing, and the oldest groups grow fastest.
  • Care is shifting home. Home Care Package use rose by about two thirds from 2021 to 2025, but spending still leans to residential care.
  • The picture is uneven. States differ in age and in care use, so a national average hides local needs.

Data note. The care-use line chart is approximate. Charts combine different organisations and years, so read differences as patterns, not exact gaps.
